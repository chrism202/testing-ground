# Leaderboard Arena research and editorial seeds

Checked November 3, 2026 (America/Los_Angeles).

Initial Elo scores are subjective editorial priors informed by published methods and public discussion. They are not a measured internet consensus or imported user votes. Your votes judge each board’s published model rankings.

The score range is deliberately modest (1120–1400). Open methods, reproducible data and independent evaluation improve the prior; opaque weighting, stale tasks, submission/harness mismatches, small samples and known disputes reduce it. These numbers are editorial judgment, not statistically inferred sentiment. Votes start at zero.

18 evaluation boards; 35 curated pairwise ranking claims. Historical snapshots, configurations, uncertainty and lower-is-better metrics are labeled in each claim.
