THE ACTUAL METHODOLOGY

Strong opinions.
Simple math.

Each leaderboard starts at 1,200 Elo. Agree or disagree with a head-to-head matchup and both scores update using K = 32. Higher-rated opponents are worth more points. For now, votes and scores stay in your browser: these are your local standings. The provider list is illustrative starter data.
